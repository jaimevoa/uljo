Manic GT

Making parking easy.

2026

The project.

A working prototype of the centre screen of the Manic GT: navigation with guidance, climate, phone, music and the car’s settings, all on one display.

It also parks itself: the screen finds a free spot, shows the cameras and guides the manoeuvre. The interface is in French.
