Track On

A voice memo designed for musicians.

2026

The project.

Track On is a phone app that helps new musicians save their ideas the second they happen: record a voice memo, then add lyrics and chords to it.

Ideas are sorted into lists, inspiration boards keep references next to the songs, and a feed lets friends listen and collaborate.

Thank you for checking Track On.
